# Can I wax or treat a client who is on her period?

Yes. Menstruation is not a contraindication for waxing, facials, massage, lash and brow work, or nails. The real questions are comfort, hygiene and how you handle the conversation — not whether you're allowed to treat.

**Comfort.** Many clients find skin more sensitive and waxing more painful in the days around their period. That is a pain-tolerance issue, not a safety one. Offer to reschedule if they would rather wait, but don't refuse or assume. It is the client's call.

**Intimate waxing (bikini, Brazilian).** This is where it needs a plan.

- Ask at booking or consultation, in neutral wording, rather than making them raise it at the couch.
- A tampon or menstrual cup is the client's choice. A string can get in the way, so agree beforehand whether they would prefer to rebook. Never insist on either.
- Keep draping, privacy and disposable couch roll in place, as you would for any intimate treatment. Dispose of anything soiled straight away, with gloves on.
- Standard infection control applies to every client anyway, so treat the situation as routine, not exceptional.

**Massage.** General massage is fine. Avoid deep abdominal or lower-back work if the client has cramps, unless they ask for it. Heavy bleeding, severe pain, or bleeding that seems unusual for them is a reason to stop and suggest they speak to their GP.

**Laser and IPL.** Sensitivity may be higher, so discuss comfort first. Your usual skin assessment and patch test still apply.

Record that you asked and what the client chose, so any therapist at the next visit knows too.
