# Can I use microcurrent or radiofrequency on a client with a pacemaker?

No — for microcurrent, it's an absolute contraindication. Microcurrent facials, EMS body toning, and galvanic treatments pass electrical current through the body, and that current can interfere with a pacemaker or implanted defibrillator (ICD). No insurer in the UK will cover you for running current through a client with a cardiac implant, and no reputable device manufacturer sanctions it.

Radiofrequency is nearly as strict. RF skin tightening generates an electromagnetic field, and most manufacturers list pacemakers and ICDs as a contraindication for that reason. Some clinics will treat a limb far from the chest with written cardiologist clearance — but for a solo salon or spa, the safe and insurable answer is don't.

What you can still offer: manual facials, most massage (check for other cardiac flags first), LED light therapy, lash and brow work, waxing, nails, and most skincare treatments are all fine. A pacemaker rules out the current, not the client.

What to do:

- **Ask about cardiac implants on every consultation form** — pacemaker, ICD, loop recorder. Clients often don't volunteer it because they don't connect a facial with their heart.
- **Record the answer**, not just the treatment decision. If a therapist covers your column next month, the flag must travel with the client.
- **Offer the alternative in the same breath** — "microcurrent isn't safe with your pacemaker, but here's what will give you a similar result" keeps the booking instead of losing it.
- **Never accept "it's fine, I've had it done before."** Previous luck is not clearance.
