How clients record and share their Beauty Passport

The Beauty Passport is a free client app. Clients keep their own beauty and safety record, choose what to share, and hand it to a salon in seconds. Here's how it works for salon owners considering SAY-OS.

Step 2

The client controls what's shared

The record belongs to the client. They decide when to share it and with whom, and can control what a salon sees. Sharing is by QR code — the same passport works in any salon, with the profile translated for the reader.

Step 3

The salon reviews it before treatment

When a client shares their passport, the salon can review the information the client has chosen to share before treatment. A visual walkthrough of the salon view is coming shortly.

Step 4

Why it helps a salon

A shared Beauty Passport gives you more context than an appointment alone provides — a client's allergies, patch tests, skin type and history, brought together in one place to review before treatment. It doesn't make decisions for you: professional judgement stays with the practitioner.
