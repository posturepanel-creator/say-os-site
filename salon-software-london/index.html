Your bookings, client records and business tools in one place.

Running a salon means keeping track of more than appointments. There are client consultations, staff schedules, products used during treatments and payments to record.

SAY brings these parts of your working day into one workspace. Clients can share their Beauty Passport, your team can review relevant information, and your business figures come from the records saved in your salon account.

How SAY fits with your tools

SAY has its own booking system and does not sync with Treatwell or Fresha. You can still use a marketplace for discovery, but bookings live in SAY.

Shared Beauty Passport records can support a consultation independently of where a booking was made. Booking-linked advisory guidance applies to supported bookings made in SAY, is advisory only, and is subject to your practitioner's assessment.
