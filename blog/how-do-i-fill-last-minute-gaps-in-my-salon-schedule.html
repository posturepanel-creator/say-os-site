How do I fill last-minute gaps in my salon's schedule?

A cancellation at 11am for a 2pm slot isn't lost money yet — it's lost only if you wait for the phone to ring. The salons that recover those hours have one thing in common: they already know exactly who to call.

Build a standing waitlist, not a vague memory. Every time a client says "I wanted Saturday but you were full," that's a name for the list. When a gap opens, you're not guessing — you're offering a slot someone already asked for. That conversation converts because you're solving her problem, not begging to fill yours.

Three habits make gaps disappear:

Reach the right people, fast. A same-day gap needs a same-minute message. The client who can come at short notice is usually the one who lives nearby or works flexibly — flag them so you call them first instead of working down the list cold.

Lead with the treatment, not the discount. "I've had a 2pm come free if you want your roots done before the weekend" beats "20% off if you fill this slot." Discounting empty slots teaches clients to wait for cancellations. Offering convenience doesn't.

Track the no-shows separately. A gap from a genuine cancellation is bad luck. A gap from a repeat no-show is a pattern worth managing with deposits.
