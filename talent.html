How it works

Three steps. You decide each one.

01

Create your profile

Your skills, experience level, availability, how you like to work, and your qualifications.

02

Salons send a request

A salon that needs your skills sends you a request through SAY. You see who they are and what they're looking for.

03

You decide

Accept to talk, go for an interview or a trial day, and if it's right, join their team in the app. Decline, and nothing is shared.

Your profile, your terms

Salons see enough to ask. Not more.

Before you accept a request, a salon sees your initials, your skills, your experience level, your availability and your area — not your name, not your contact details, not your qualifications.

  • Your name and contact details are shared only when you accept
  • Qualifications and academy are shared only when you accept
  • Decline a request and the salon sees nothing further
  • No fee, no agency, no percentage of your pay
