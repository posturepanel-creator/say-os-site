Pricing questions

Is there really a free plan?
Yes. You can start free with the dashboard, calendar, up to 25 clients, 50 bookings a month and 1 team member, and the client Beauty Passport — no credit card and no time limit. Upgrade to Standard when you need more.
What does Standard cost?
£79 per month. The Founding plan is £234 every 6 months, billed upfront (the same as £39 a month), and renews at that price for as long as your subscription stays active — limited to the first 20 salons. Both give you the full platform with up to 500 clients and unlimited bookings.
What's included in Standard?
Up to 500 clients, unlimited bookings, up to 5 active team members, the voice assistant, recorded client information to review (contraindications, allergies, patch tests), consultation records, stock and inventory, WhatsApp messaging, and reports. The talent marketplace is launching soon and is not included yet.
How do I pay?
You start free on the web, then upgrade inside the app, where Stripe handles payment securely. You can cancel any time.
