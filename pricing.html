Pricing questions

Is there really a free plan?
Yes. You can start free with the dashboard, calendar, up to 25 clients, and the client Beauty Passport — no credit card and no time limit. Upgrade to Standard when you need more.
What does Standard cost?
£79 per month, or £234 for six months paid up front — the same as £39 a month. Both give you the full platform with unlimited clients.
What's included in Standard?
Unlimited clients, the voice assistant, full safety intelligence (contraindications, allergies, patch tests), consultation records, stock and inventory, WhatsApp messaging, and reports. The talent marketplace is launching soon and is not included yet.
How do I pay?
You start free on the web, then upgrade inside the app, where Stripe handles payment securely. You can cancel any time.
