Questions

Can clients share their Passport with a salon that doesn't use SAY-OS?
Yes. By QR code or link; the salon opens it in a browser without an account, after a consent screen. Links expire after 90 days and the client can switch a link off at any time.
Can clients book directly with my salon?
Yes, once connected — you invite them or they invite you. Requests arrive in your calendar for you to confirm. You can switch on instant confirmation for new or returning clients in your settings. No booking commission.
What's included in the free plan?
The free plan includes your calendar, up to 25 clients, 50 bookings a month and 1 team member, plus client invitations and CSV import. Voice booking, see-who's-due rebooking, Instagram publishing, stock and suppliers, and insights and reports are on the Standard plan. See pricing.
