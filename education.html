Starting out

Two ways to start.

Graduates choose their path on SAY — or take both.

  • Run your own business

    Get three months of the Standard plan free with a student code provided through your academy, entered at checkout. Manage bookings, client records, voice booking and reports as you start your own business.

  • Be found by salons

    Create a professional profile that salons on SAY can discover and send requests to. Discovery shows experience level and badges. Your qualifications and academy are shared after you accept a salon's request.
