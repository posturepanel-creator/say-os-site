How does SAY-OS compare
to everything else?

Most salon software handles bookings and marketing. SAY-OS adds what they often don't: book straight into your calendar by voice while your hands are busy, plus a client-controlled Beauty Passport. This page sets SAY-OS beside the main UK platforms and sticks to what each vendor documents.

SAY-OS is a voice-first salon operating system with a free client Beauty Passport. It handles bookings, inventory, client records and a timestamped consultation record the salon can download, with voice-first booking at its core. SAY-OS checks 12 conditions and life stages and records the products a client uses at home, and is ICO registered (ZC121398), with structured handling of client health information. Other platforms document safety records too — Fresha and Phorest both document patch-test recording (see Sources).

SAY-OS has its own calendar and bookings and does not sync with other systems. Clients book directly into the SAY calendar, fill in their Beauty Passport once, and (planned) can receive care reminders based on their own routine cycle. Start free with dashboard, calendar, and up to 25 clients and 50 bookings a month. Standard plan is £79/month.
