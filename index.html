Beauty Passport

Your next appointment.
With more of the picture.

A returning client can bring something new: a skincare routine, a medication, an allergy or a treatment at another clinic. Their Beauty Passport gives them a place to maintain that information and choose which categories to share before an appointment.

Example · shared before an appointment
Beauty historyShared
Allergies & conditionsShared
MedicationsHidden by client
Home skincareShared
LinkExpires in 90 days · revocable

Beauty history

Recorded treatments, colour formulas and preferences.

Health and safety information

Client-reported allergies, medications, conditions and patch-test information.

Home skincare

Products and ingredients that may be relevant to the next treatment.

Sharing is client-controlled, with category choices and revocable links. Practitioners review shared information and confirm relevant changes during the consultation. For supported treatments booked in SAY-OS, available Safety Profile information can prompt advisory guidance — missing information or no flag is not treatment clearance.

Compatibility

Can I use SAY-OS alongside another booking system?

Yes. SAY-OS has its own calendar and bookings. It can be used alongside another system, but it does not sync with Treatwell or Fresha.
