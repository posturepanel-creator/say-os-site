The real cost of running a salon

Four problems draining your business every week.

01

Missed bookings add up fast

Enquiries arrive while every pair of hands is busy. Each one that goes unanswered is a client who books elsewhere.

02

No-shows cost you twice

The slot earns nothing, and the stylist is paid anyway. Without deposits and reminders, you absorb both.

03

Hiring eats your week

Posting, sifting, messaging, chasing references, trial days that go nowhere. The time comes out of your own column.

04

Lapsed clients are silent revenue

Many clients who stop visiting would come back if someone reached out. They're not unhappy; life got busy and nobody reminded them you exist.

What SAY-OS does

One platform. The whole salon in one place.

Client safety records

Contraindications, allergies and patch tests recorded so relevant information is reviewed before an appointment. ICO registered.

Beauty Passport

Each client's preferences, history and photos, shared with your team by the client. Clients feel remembered. New staff deliver a personal service from day one.

Consultation records

Digital consultation forms and full treatment history, searchable, with structured handling of health information.

Skin intelligence
